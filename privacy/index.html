Photo Editor

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026 · Last updated: November 26, 2026
In short: Photo Editor edits your photos on your device. Your photos, edited images and face data never leave your phone. The app has no accounts, shows no ads and does not track you. The only data that leaves the device is limited technical diagnostics sent to Google by the on-device ML Kit library, as described in Section 4.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the Photo Editor: Cutout & Blur Android app (“Photo Editor”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. It applies to the app distributed on Google Play under the package name app.cutoutblur.editor. By using the app you agree to this Policy.

2. What the app does on your device

Photo Editor lets you enhance photos, cut out a person, blur the background, smooth skin, crop, rotate, apply filters and adjustments, add text, and create collages, long or wide stitched images, free-form layouts and template designs from your photos.

  • Only the photos you pick. You choose photos in Android’s system photo picker. The app can read only the photos you select and does not ask for permission to access your whole photo library.
  • All editing happens locally. Every edit is processed on your device. We do not upload your photos, edited images or thumbnails, and we do not operate servers that receive them.
  • Face and body processing stays on the device. Person cutout, background blur and skin smoothing use Google ML Kit on your device to find the outline of a person and the position of a face in the photo you are editing. These results are used only to apply the edit you asked for. They are not stored after the edit, are not used to identify anyone, and never leave your device.
  • Saved images stay with you. Images you save go to your device’s gallery (Pictures/Photo Editor). The app also keeps small thumbnails and a list of recently saved images in its private storage so it can show them under “My Artworks”.

4. Information collected by Google ML Kit

The app uses Google ML Kit for on-device face detection and selfie segmentation. According to Google, ML Kit may send limited technical data to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics of the ML Kit service, such as:

  • Device information (for example manufacturer, model and operating system version) and app information (package name and app version)
  • Device or other identifiers used for diagnostics
  • Performance metrics, API configuration, input and output sizes, feature version, event type and error codes

This data does not include your photos or face data. Google states that ML Kit encrypts this data in transit (HTTPS) and does not transfer it to third parties. When you first use cutout, blur or smoothing, Google Play services may also download the required model to your device. This collection is governed by Google’s policies:

  • ML Kit data disclosure
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Information we collect ourselves

We do not collect any personal information through the app. There is no account, sign-in or registration, no advertising, no advertising ID, and no analytics, crash reporting or tracking of our own. We do not sell or share personal information.

If you contact us by email (for example via “Feedback” in the app), we will receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to respond to you and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.

If you tap “Share With Friends”, Android’s share sheet opens with a short text, and you decide which app, if any, receives it.

The app stores a few things locally on your device, such as the thumbnails and the list of recently saved images mentioned in Section 2. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

8. Children’s privacy

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we handle, such as feedback emails, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
